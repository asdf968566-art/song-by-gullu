Privacy

This page covers the Sangeet music app (Android and this website) and Sangeet Shorts, the tool that uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's YouTube channel.

  • Sangeet Shorts uses Google sign-in with the YouTube scope only to upload videos to the owner's own channel and to read that channel's name. It does not read, store or share anyone else's Google or YouTube data. The sign-in is stored encrypted and can be removed at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections.
  • The Sangeet app keeps your library, likes and settings on your device. If "Help improve suggestions" is on, it sends anonymous listening counts (under a random id, no name, account or location) to improve suggestions; you can turn it off in Settings. Problem reports contain phone details, never songs, files or accounts.
  • No data is sold or used for ads.
